About Me

Motivated researcher seeking new opportunities

I am an Astrophysics and Biotechnology student at the Rochester Institute of Technology with a foundation in computer science, programming, and geospatial analysis. I have experience developing GIS models to analyze environmental challenges such as marine pollution and disaster prediction. My work focuses on using data, programming (Python, C++), and spatial tools (ArcGIS, QGIS) to create solutions that support sustainable development and scientific research. Through projects, leadership in NSBE, and research experience, I am passionate about applying technology to real-world problems at the intersection of environmental science, space, and innovation. I am currently seeking opportunities in biotechnology, space research, and data-driven environmental analysis.

In my spare time, I have directly addressed a gap in accessibility and awareness, providing members with tangible resources to support their academic and professional growth
